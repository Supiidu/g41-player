# Changelog

The v0.9.0 entry is a preview awaiting on-device testing, not a stable release. Historical entries below are reconstructed from the ZIP snapshots supplied by the project owner. Their commit dates record publication to this repository, not original development dates.

- **v0.10.0-beta4 — AOD restoration, PIN isolation and album-color mini-wave (test branch):** recreate the compact window on a DOZE/DOZE_SUSPEND display after expanded closes even when PowerManager reports non-interactive; detect the ROM's primary PIN bouncer state via read-only `KeyguardStateControllerImpl.notifyPrimaryBouncerShowing(boolean)` and temporarily make the custom compact overlay invisible while preserving state; reuse expanded CAVA's artwork accent extraction and translucent vertical gradient for the compact wave. Preserves beta3 audio handoff, user-approved 0.80 treble default, music-only Compose suppression, safe toggles and fail-open behavior.
- **v0.10.0-beta3 — Visualizer handoff + stable compact media (test branch):** fixes live-device reports of compact FFT starving expanded waves by releasing its Visualizer before the expanded capture starts; keeps native media chip invisible during expansion; retains the compact window (without FFT processing) while the screen is off to preserve suppression when paused; listens to the parent Compose entry point to rearm even when the native child was previously hidden. Opt-in settings unchanged and fail-open guards preserved. Requires G41 retesting.
- **v0.10.0-beta2 — Native Media Chip Visibility (test branch):** matches the supplied Infinity X SystemUI's separate collapsed-media `AnimatedVisibility(boolean,...)` call, leaving expanded content's `AnimatedVisibility(MutableTransitionState,...)` untouched. When the custom compact bar is already on screen and `g41_glass_compact_hide_native` is explicitly set to 1, requests native media chip invisibility through the normal Compose transition; fails open if conditions differ. Never hides an entire AndroidComposeView. No device validation yet.
- **v0.10.0-beta1 — Compact InfinityBar Preview (draft):** opt-in standalone translucent compact music bar inspired by the supplied Figma; dynamic artwork/title/artist, transport play/pause, real blue FFT wave, touch-to-expand, adjustable positioning and safe Compose read-only hook. Original native compact chip and non-media events left untouched. Needs G41 on-device validation and alignment.
- **v0.9.0-beta4 — Smooth Treble CAVA HD (test branch):** better real treble transients (FFT per-band peak/RMS blend and gentler high-frequency threshold), bounded treble tilt, light neighboring-band spread, slower treble release, 5-tap spatial smoothing and monotone cubic curve drawing instead of sharp quadratic peaks. Preserves bass/mids, Monet, bottom position, album color and full disappearance when paused. Adds configurable `g41_glass_cava_treble_boost` (default 0.80 after owner validation, range 0.7–2.0) and JVM regression cases.
- **v0.9.0-beta3 — Balanced CAVA Spectrum (test branch):** mirrored mono frequency layout (bass center, treble at edges), bounded low/mid/treble adaptive EQ, logarithmic 35–12000 Hz Android FFT extraction with RMS-per-band aggregation, sparse-bin interpolation, and automated standalone JVM tests. Retains Monet, album color, card position and disappearance on pause.
- **v0.9.0-beta2 — Single CAVA HD Wave (test branch):** replaces two translucent fluid layers with one album-colored filled 2D FFT silhouette, 144 smooth display samples, faster response and no bottom resting stripe; preserves Monet glass and lower placement. RMS fallback displays loudness only, not simulated spectral peaks.
- **v0.9.0-beta1 — Monet & Lower Waves (test branch):** dynamic wallpaper palettes for glass/labels/buttons/progress, original album colors for waves, lower adjustable card placement, edge-to-edge bottom waves completely hidden on pause.
- **v0.8.1 — Fluid Wave:** filled continuous waveform with interpolation and album-reactive colors; preserves existing audio capture modes.
- **v0.8.0 — Compact:** experimental hiding of the compact InfinityBar while the expanded overlay is visible; adds `CompactBarVisibility`.
- **v0.7.0 — CAVA:** FFT-driven CAVA-style spectrum bar visualization.
- **v0.6.0 — Live Spectrum:** real audio spectrum and album artwork palette.
- **v0.5.2 — ClockStyle:** additional SystemUI lockscreen clock detection.
- **v0.5.1 — Clock Fix:** safer clock view discovery and diagnostics.
- **v0.5.0 — Live Waves:** music-reactive expanded card visualizations.
- **v0.4.0 — Figma:** expanded media interface and wallpaper-backed design.
- **v0.3.0 — Custom Panel:** experimental native-style media panel and opt-in toggle.
- **v0.2.0 — Shape Test:** tightly scoped Compose card shape hook experiment.
- **v0.1.0 — Diagnostic Starter:** Vector/Xposed hook diagnostics without visual replacement.

See `docs/history/README-v*.md` and the source commits for details. A duplicated copy of the v0.6 ZIP had identical SHA-256 contents and was not published as a separate version.
